<WhyItMatters>
  [Lessons 9 and 10](/04-context-and-memory/10-deciding-what-to-remember/00-intro/)
  made sure the store is scoped, and that what goes in is true and allowed.
  They didn't stop it from growing forever, and a store that grows forever
  gets worse. Routine notes crowd out what matters, old facts outlive their
  truth, and an agent that follows what it recalls starts following clutter.
  This lesson keeps memory useful over months: the right things rise, the
  rest step aside, and nothing is lost for good unless someone asks.
</WhyItMatters>
