<WhyItMatters>
  Every technique in
  [Lessons 4](/04-context-and-memory/04-when-the-window-fills/00-intro/) and
  [5](/04-context-and-memory/05-compaction-and-summarization/00-intro/)
  makes the context smaller by throwing something away: a result's content,
  a whole round, the exact words behind a summary. That's fine for data the
  agent can fetch again. It isn't fine for a snapshot that will never be the
  same twice, or for a detail whose importance only shows up ten steps
  later.

  This lesson moves what the agent has seen out of the context instead of
  out of existence. Large results go to a store the moment they arrive.
  Cleared results and compacted rounds go there too. What the agent learns
  along the way goes into notes of its own. The context stays small, and
  everything in it stays one call away from the full detail.
</WhyItMatters>
