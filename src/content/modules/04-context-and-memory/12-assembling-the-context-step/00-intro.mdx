<WhyItMatters>
  Each lesson in this module solved one problem on its own page. A real
  agent has several of those problems at once, in one loop: a history that
  won't fit, facts that have to survive compaction, tools that shouldn't
  all be sent, and a user who comes back next week. This lesson puts the
  pieces in one place and measures the result. As it turned out, assembling
  them surfaced problems that no single lesson could show.
</WhyItMatters>
