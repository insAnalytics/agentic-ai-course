<WhyItMatters>
  Every exercise since Lesson 1 has used a bare `assert` as its grading
  mechanism — this lesson makes that real: the actual framework, the actual
  discipline of enumerating failure modes deliberately, and the actual
  tools (mocking, fixtures, coverage) that make a test suite trustworthy
  rather than just present. An agent backend that isn't tested isn't safely
  changeable — and given where this course is headed later, a test suite
  is also what lets an AI coding assistant verify its own changes actually
  work, not just that they look right.
</WhyItMatters>
