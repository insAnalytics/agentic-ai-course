<WhyItMatters>
  Once an agent is in use, its requests stop coming from a suite. Nobody
  writes down the right answers, the traffic drifts, and every change
  meets people before anyone has measured it on them. Monitoring is how
  the evaluation continues. In this lesson's simulated traffic, a
  dashboard showed Module 6's layered checks withholding half of the first
  ten answers, with no grading at all; a two-window alert caught a
  regression a single window couldn't separate from chance; and a
  before/after comparison blamed a change that did nothing in three weeks
  out of four, where a canary beside a control stayed quiet.
</WhyItMatters>
