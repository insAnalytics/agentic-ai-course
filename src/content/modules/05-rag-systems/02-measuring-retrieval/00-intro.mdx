<WhyItMatters>
  A retrieval failure doesn't crash. It hands the model the wrong passages,
  and the model writes a fluent answer from them. Every technique in the rest
  of this module, from chunking to reranking to agentic search, claims to
  retrieve better, and some of those claims won't hold on this corpus. This
  lesson builds the instrument that decides, before any technique is added.
</WhyItMatters>
