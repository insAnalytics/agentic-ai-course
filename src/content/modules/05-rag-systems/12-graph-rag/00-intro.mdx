<WhyItMatters>
  Some questions are about structure: what depends on what, and what patterns
  run through a whole collection. The answer is spread across passages that
  don't resemble the question, or across every document at once. A graph built
  from the documents answers them exactly, with every link cited. It's also
  one of the most expensive indexes to build and keep current, and when the
  relationships already exist as data, it's the wrong tool.
</WhyItMatters>
