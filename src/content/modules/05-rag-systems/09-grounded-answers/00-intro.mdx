<WhyItMatters>
  Everything earlier in this module improves what reaches the model. This
  lesson is about what the model does with it, and what the system does
  with the reply. An answer that cites nothing, cites what it wasn't given,
  invents what the sources don't say, or repeats a stale page can't be told
  apart from a good one by reading it. The checks here are cheap and
  mechanical, and they're the first layer of trust, before the verification
  Module 6 adds.
</WhyItMatters>
