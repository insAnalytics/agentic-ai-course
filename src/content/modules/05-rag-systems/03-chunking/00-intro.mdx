<WhyItMatters>
  Chunking decides the unit that every later step works with: what gets
  embedded, what gets scored, what gets sent to the model and what gets
  cited. It happens once, at indexing time, so its mistakes are invisible at
  query time: a fact cut in half or a section truncated by the embedder
  simply never comes back. Getting it right is mostly about avoiding the bad
  choices, and about measuring the rest fairly.
</WhyItMatters>
