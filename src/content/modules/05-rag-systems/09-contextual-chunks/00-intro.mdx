<WhyItMatters>
  Chunking cuts a document into pieces, and the words that say what a piece
  is about are often left behind in a title or a heading. Every lesson in
  this module lost questions that way before this one. Putting that context
  back was the largest single improvement to the pipeline: from 32 to 36
  questions answered in the top five, and from 22 to 26 at rank 1. It also
  changes what's indexed, what indexing costs, and what must happen when a
  document changes.
</WhyItMatters>
