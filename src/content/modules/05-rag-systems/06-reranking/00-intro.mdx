<WhyItMatters>
  An agent usually sends only a handful of retrieved chunks to its model, so
  what matters most is whether the answer is among the first few. Reranking
  is the most direct way to improve that, and on this module's questions it
  did: more answers at rank 1 from every first stage. It also adds latency
  to every question, and its errors are different from the first stage's, so
  it's a component to measure, not to switch on and forget.
</WhyItMatters>
