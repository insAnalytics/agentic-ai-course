<WhyItMatters>
  Nearly everything the rest of this course does with meaning — finding
  relevant context for a question, comparing whether two pieces of text are
  really saying the same thing, the eventual RAG pipeline this lesson laid
  groundwork for — comes down to embeddings and the cosine similarity
  between them. Getting the token-embedding/text-embedding distinction
  genuinely clear now, rather than papering over it, is what prevents real
  confusion later when both start showing up in the same conversation about
  a real system.
</WhyItMatters>
