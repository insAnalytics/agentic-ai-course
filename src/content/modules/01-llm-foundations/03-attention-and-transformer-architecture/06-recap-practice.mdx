<Subsection title="Closing synthesis — tracing one token through the full architecture" variant="a">

This isn't graded — it's a chance to see every mechanism this lesson
covered as one connected pipeline, rather than five separate ideas.

Follow `"bank"` in `"I sat by the river bank"` through the full
pipeline, one step at a time:

1. **Token embedding lookup** — `"bank"` starts as a fixed vector,
   [looked up purely from its token ID](/01-llm-foundations/03-attention-and-transformer-architecture/01-why-a-token-needs-context/),
   identical to what `"bank"` would get in any other sentence at this
   stage.
2. **Positional encoding added** — that fixed vector gets combined with
   a vector [specific to `"bank"`'s position in this exact sentence](/01-llm-foundations/03-attention-and-transformer-architecture/03-positional-encoding/),
   making its starting representation already slightly different than
   an identical word at a different position would get.
3. **Layer 1: multi-head attention** —
   [several attention computations run in parallel](/01-llm-foundations/03-attention-and-transformer-architecture/04-stacking-layers-into-a-transformer/),
   each comparing `"bank"`'s Query against every other token's Key —
   `"river"` scores highly relevant, pulling `"bank"`'s representation
   toward a riverside meaning,
   [exactly as computed directly in the attention-mechanism concept's demo](/01-llm-foundations/03-attention-and-transformer-architecture/02-the-attention-mechanism/).
4. **Layer 1: feedforward** — that attention output gets further
   transformed by a feedforward network, completing one full transformer
   block.
5. **Layers 2 through N** — the same two-step process repeats, each
   layer refining an already-contextualized representation rather than
   starting over from the raw embedding — by the final layer, `"bank"`'s
   representation reflects not just `"river"`'s direct influence, but
   several rounds of progressively refined context.
6. **(If this were an MoE model)** —
   [a router would have selected only a subset of that layer's experts](/01-llm-foundations/03-attention-and-transformer-architecture/05-mixture-of-experts/)
   to actually process `"bank"`'s representation, rather than using every
   parameter in the layer.
